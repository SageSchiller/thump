# Thump

A 16-pad drum machine and beat maker for the m-vave SMC-PAD Pocket, running in a Chromium
app window. Works with the pad over USB or Bluetooth (both show up as normal MIDI ports on
Linux), plus mouse and keyboard.

## Run

    thump            # or "Thump" in the app launcher

The launcher (`./thump`) opens Chromium with its own profile in `~/.local/share/thump/browser`,
lets sound start without a click, stops Chromium slowing the app down when its window is in the
background, and asks Bluetooth to connect the pad if it is switched on. Chromium asks once to
let Thump control MIDI devices (needed for the pad lights) and the microphone the first time you
record: choose Allow.

## What it does

- **318 sounds**: 86 original synth sounds (dubstep, riddim and neuro basses, industrial metal
  and noise, builds, risers, drops, pads) made in `synth.js`, and 232 drum samples from 12
  freely licensed Hydrogen kits (see CREDITS.md, generated by the build).
- **13 ready-made kits** (Dubstep, Riddim / neuro, Audiophob dubstep, Industrial, Factory,
  Electro, Trap, Drum machines, Hand drums, Heavy metal, Rock, Hip-hop, Classic 808).
- **Per pad**: sound, volume, pitch, length, drive, pan, hold, reverse, cut group.
- **Patterns and songs**: 8 patterns (A to H) of 1, 2 or 4 bars. Click a pattern while playing
  and it switches at the end of the pass. Chain patterns into a song (A ×2, B ×4, ...).
- **Recording**: count-in, metronome, snap, undo per pass, note repeat, click-to-edit grid.
- **Groove and mix**: swing, sidechain pump (on kicks or every beat), master filter and reverb,
  mute rows (or "Mute with pads" to drop parts out live).
- **Notes mode**: one sound on all 16 pads as a scale, for basslines.
- **Wobble designer**: speed (with a different speed for the second half of the bar), shape,
  wave, filter range, resonance, growl, grit, sub and width. Designs follow the tempo.
- **Your own sounds**: drop audio files on a pad, record with the microphone (silence is
  trimmed), or chop a long sound across the pads (by detected hits or 16 equal slices).
- **Pad lights** (SMC-PAD Pocket over USB): each pad lights in its sound's color, flashes when
  it plays (by hand or from the beat) and goes dark when muted or empty. Thump never saves to the
  pad: its own colors come back when Thump closes, when the lights are turned off in Settings, or
  when the pad is switched off.
- **Save audio**: render a pattern (×1 to ×8) or the whole song to MP3 or WAV, faster than
  real time. Beats save in the app and export to `.thump.json` (with your own sounds inside).

Esc stops all sound. Space plays and stops. Ctrl+Z undoes.

## Pad layout

Pad 1 is bottom left (note 36), counting left to right, bottom row up to pad 16 top right
(note 51). Pads send on MIDI channel 10. Any note is folded onto the 16 pads, so pad banks
still work.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`: the app
- `synth.js`: synth sound library and wobble designer (rendered offline with Web Audio)
- `presets.js`: the ready-made kits
- `kits.js`: generated sample library (base64 Ogg Opus), built by `build_kits.py`
- `sources/`: Hydrogen kit archives used by the build (Hydrogen itself is not needed)
- `vendor/lamejs.js`: MP3 encoder (LGPL, `vendor/lamejs-LICENSE.txt`)
- `tools/cdp.mjs`: headless Chromium test driver

## Build and test

    python3 build_kits.py      # rebuild kits.js and CREDITS.md (needs ffmpeg)
    node tools/cdp.mjs "file://$PWD/index.html?selftest" --wait 1000 --eval \
      "new Promise(r => { const t = setInterval(() => { if (document.title.startsWith('SELFTEST')) { clearInterval(t); r(document.getElementById('selftest').textContent); } }, 500); })"

`build_kits.py` also builds optional extra kits into `kits-extra.js` from archives in
`sources/extra/`, for kits from Hydrogen's library that don't state a license. Neither is in
this repository; the app loads `kits-extra.js` if it is there.

The self-test (159 checks) checks every preset sound exists, renders every synth sound and 15
wobble designs, decodes every sample, and checks loop timing, swing, song order, pattern
switching, old beat files, offline rendering, WAV and MP3 encoding, mic trimming, the
chopper's hit detection, and the pad lights against a pretend pad. `--fake-media` gives headless Chromium a fake microphone; `--shot
file.png` saves a screenshot.

## How the pad lights work

The pad ignores MIDI notes sent to it. Its colors live in the settings memory that M-VAVE's
editor app changes with SysEx on the USB "Private" port, using a protocol reverse-engineered by
[spectalive/smc-pad](https://github.com/spectalive/smc-pad) for the bigger SMC-PAD; the Pocket
uses the same protocol. Its memory holds 4 presets of 2931 bytes, each 112 pad records (7 banks
of 16) of 26 bytes, with the pad's red, green and blue at bytes 5 to 7. The pad only takes writes
after a full read of its settings, kept alive by reading its 12-byte global block twice a second.
Over Bluetooth the same settings are reached through a separate GATT service (`AE40`), which
Thump doesn't use yet.
